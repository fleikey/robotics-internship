With ±2m/s commands I expect the Go2 to fall more often for fast commands, most probably during the early training, but perhaps for the whole training as well. Since to move twice as fast, the robot would need to either make longer steps or/and increase the frequency of the steps, which makes the robot unstable: A robot is statically stable while its center of mass sits above the support polygon, the area enclosed by the feet on the ground. At slow speeds, three or more feet are often down, so it's naturally stable. At high speeds, fewer feet touch the ground at once, and sometimes none do. The robot then depends on dynamic stability: staying upright through momentum and well-timed foot placement, which is less forgiving of mistakes. 

Although I have stated that the robot might fall throughout the training, with the increase of iterations by 3x, the robot should learn to fall less often than before. If the success rate increases by a noticable margin, the issue would be undertraining and it can be likely solved with more training.
